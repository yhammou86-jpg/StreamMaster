⚡ Cached beats everything

Anything your debrid service already holds is ranked first and plays instantly — no swarm, no buffering.

👥 Liveness over hype

A 4K REMUX with one seeder is not better than a 1080p with ninety. Seed count is scored as a penalty, not a tie-breaker.

💎 Parsed, not guessed

Release names are decoded into resolution, source, codec, HDR, audio and group — then rendered as structured lines.

Pipeline

Four stages, one request

Every stream request runs the same deterministic pipeline under a hard time budget. Nothing waits on a slow tracker — whatever arrives by the deadline gets ranked and returned.

01 / RESOLVE

Identify the title

The IMDb id from your client is resolved through Cinemeta into a title, year and episode — cached for twelve hours.

02 / FAN OUT

Scrape in parallel

Enabled sources fire simultaneously with per-source timeouts. A dead tracker costs you nothing but its own slot.

03 / PROBE

One cache call

Every unique infohash goes to your debrid service in a single request. Cached results jump to the top.

04 / RANK

Score and format

Resolution, source, codec, seeders and cache state combine into one score. Duplicates merge across trackers.

Engineering

Built for the free tier

Most addons fall over on serverless because they assume a long-running process. Hydra is designed around the opposite constraint.

⚡

Batch cache probing

A hundred hashes checked in one HTTP call instead of a hundred. The single biggest speed win a debrid API allows.

🔗

Lazy resolution

Stream URLs point at Hydra, not at your debrid account. The direct link is minted the instant you press play.

🌊

Native P2P

Uncached results return an infohash plus trackers. Your client does the peer work, so Hydra never proxies video.

📦

Zero dependencies

No SDK, no axios, no cheerio. Pure Node built-ins: a ~50ms cold start and nothing to break on a supply-chain update.

🔒

Opaque config URLs

Set one environment variable and your entire configuration — API key included — is AES-256-GCM encrypted inside the link.

🩺

Source diagnostics

Trackers are blocked unevenly by network. A built-in probe tells you which sources actually answer for you, then you keep those.

Your choice

Debrid, P2P, or both

You should not have to pick a side. Hydra runs whichever path makes sense for each result, and you decide how far it is allowed to go.

Hybrid default

Cached results stream from your debrid service instantly. Everything else is handed to your client as P2P.

  • Maximum number of playable results
  • Cached first, peers as backup
  • Best choice for most people

Debrid only fastest

Only show what your service can serve right now. No buffering, no peers, no waiting on a swarm.

  • Instant start on every result
  • Zero bandwidth from your connection
  • Needs an active debrid plan

P2P only no account

No debrid service is ever contacted. Every result is an infohash and your client streams straight from the swarm.

  • Works with no subscription at all
  • Nothing stored on any server
  • Your IP is visible to peers

Ready in one minute

Pick a debrid service or skip it, test your sources, and Hydra generates a personal addon URL. Paste it into Stremio or Nuvio and you are done.
